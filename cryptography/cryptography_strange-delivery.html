"Strange Delivery"

Cryptography

Description

You recieved an unexpected parcel with a string of random letters and "10:00" written on it. Decipher the message on the parcel to know its true meaning.

Message:

wOOd_WO_sx-YWkCrE

Flag Format:

HACKFAX{deciphered_message}

Solution

    1. Caesar Cipher (shift of 10 [a->k]):
      wOOd_WO_sx-YWkCrE >>> mEEt_ME_in-OMaShU

Flag

HACKFAX{mEEt_ME_in-OMaShU}
